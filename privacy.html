Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 24 September 2025

BridalBooker Machine (BBM) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our lead management app, designed exclusively for makeup artists (MUAs) across Tamil Nadu.

Information We Collect

  • User Profile: We collect basic information such as your name, contact details, and professional credentials when you register as an MUA.
  • Lead Data: We process information related to leads, including booking status and claim history.
  • Usage Data: We collect data on app usage, such as lead claims, locks, and interactions via WhatsApp.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our lead-sharing platform.
  • To manage lead claim and lock policies as described in our Terms & Conditions.
  • To facilitate communication between MUAs and potential clients via WhatsApp (phone numbers are never displayed).
  • To ensure fair access and visibility of leads among MUAs.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

  • We do not share your personal information with third parties except as required by law or with your explicit consent.
  • Lead contact details are never displayed publicly; communication is only possible through the provided WhatsApp icon.
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable data may be used for analytics and service improvement.

Data Security

  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
  • Access to your account is protected by authentication mechanisms.

Your Rights

  • You may access, update, or delete your profile information at any time via the app.
  • For any privacy-related requests, please contact us at contact@lokiwebvibe.com.

Children's Privacy

BBM is intended for professional makeup artists. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on our website.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@lokiwebvibe.com.
