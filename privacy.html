Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 5 November 2026

Loki Partnerz (BridalBooker Machine, or BBM) provides lead-management services to makeup artists. This policy describes the information handled when you use our apps and service.

Information We Collect

  • Account and profile information: username, password credentials, phone number, contact-person and business names, service category, and profile links or other details you provide.
  • Lead and service information: client names, phone numbers, event details, lead claims, booking status, notes, and related service activity needed to operate the lead-management service.
  • Device and app information: push-notification token and app interaction events used to deliver notifications, operate, and understand use of the service.
  • Purchase information: Apple transaction identifiers or Razorpay transaction identifiers, amount, and plan details used to verify a purchase and grant the purchased access. Apple or Razorpay processes the payment; BBM receives transaction information needed to manage the purchase, not your complete payment credentials.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure accounts, provide lead-management features, and enforce lead-claim limits.
  • To show relevant client lead details to eligible artists as needed to provide the service and enable contact by phone or WhatsApp.
  • To process and verify Apple in-app purchases on iOS and Razorpay payments on Android, provide the purchased access, and respond to support requests.
  • To deliver push notifications and understand app usage so we can operate and improve the service.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

  • We use service providers to operate the service, including Firebase for push notifications, Meta App Events for app interaction analytics, WhatsApp to deliver password-recovery messages, email providers for service notifications, Apple for iOS App Store purchases, and Razorpay for Android payments. These providers process information under their own privacy terms.
  • Client lead information is shown to eligible artists within the service for lead follow-up; it is not intended as public directory information.
  • We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect users and the service.

Data Security

  • We use access controls and authentication to protect account and service data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your Rights

  • You may review and update profile information in the app. On iOS, use Settings → Delete Account to request deletion. If you cannot access the app, contact us using the email below.
  • When account deletion completes, we remove the account, profile, access credentials, subscription entitlements, and account-linked records from active service tables. We retain a deletion timestamp without account identifiers and keyed HMACs of Apple transaction identifiers, without account linkage, only to prevent a purchase from being reused. These limited records cannot restore account access.
  • For any privacy-related requests, please contact us at contact@lokiwebvibe.com.

Children's Privacy

BBM is intended for professional users and is not directed to children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on our website.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@lokiwebvibe.com.
