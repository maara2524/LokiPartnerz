Loki Partnerz Support

Loki Partnerz (BridalBooker Machine) is a lead-management service for makeup artists. This page is the support entry point for the Loki Partnerz mobile apps.

Contact us

Email: contact@lokiwebvibe.com
We usually reply within one business day.

Subscriptions

iOS plans are purchased in the app through Apple's in-app purchase system. They provide fixed-term access and do not renew automatically. Access is connected to your Loki Partnerz account. Android plans are purchased in the Android app. Sign in to the same account to check your active access. For purchase or account help, contact us at the email above.

Delete your account

Signed-in users can delete their account from the app: Settings → Delete Account. You can also email us from the address linked to your account and ask us to delete it.
